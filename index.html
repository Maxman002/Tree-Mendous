Climate through time

A longer view of Heilbronn’s climate.

Explore annual climate indicators from the supplied regional datasets. These historical figures are separate from the bike-sensor readings on the map; they are not street-level measurements or forecasts. The profile PDFs also include modeled RCP 4.5 and RCP 8.5 projections to 2100; those scenarios are kept separate from this historical chart.

The selected indicator may vary substantially year to year. The displayed change compares the first and last available annual records, not a trend estimate.

Erlenbach profile at a glance

The supplied LUBW profile reports a mean temperature of 10.9 °C for 1996–2025, 1.5 °C above its 1961–1990 reference mean (9.4 °C). Its 2025 mean is 11.2 °C. These period summaries are not an annual time series.
